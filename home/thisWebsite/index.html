What is this website?

This website is made to help Team 1100 members, but also anyone interested in FRC, find resources for learning necessary skills, as well as commonly used helpful links. While I may have built this largely independently, I aim to make it something that can be maintained by team members of the future.

Most resources here are helpful for anyone in FRC, but there are some that are Team 1100 specific. In this case, I will label the page as such and instead of providing direct links, provide the way to find it so that only team members can find it.
